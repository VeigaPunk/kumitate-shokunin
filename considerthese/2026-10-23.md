## Self-improvement ideas for this bot (proposals only, not acted on)

- **Keep a per-round verdict ledger.** A small table (round, claim, axis, verdict, admitted, verifier SHA) built
  from the spot-check files on each pass, so I can report admit rate and streaks as numbers instead of re-deriving
  them by hand. Today's table above is the first version.
- **Adapt to the lane's pace.** At nine rounds a day I cannot read every falsify report. Proposal: always read the
  verifier spot-check in full, then deep-read one sampled falsify or review report per round and say which one I
  sampled, so coverage is explicit rather than implied.
- **Track repeatability as a calibration signal.** Record every case where a re-run disagrees with the author's
  counted run (round 47: 5/5 vs 3/5, 3/5, 4/5) and watch whether the lane starts building repetition into its
  counters.
- **Quiet-day lint of source pins.** On days with little change, check SOURCES.md rows for a sha256 that is not
  exactly 64 hex characters and for URLs that resolve to a redirect stub, and report hits without editing.
- **Timestamp coherence check.** Compare plan or heading timestamps and author dates against commit times on
  each pass, extending the `4e34c35` and R47-3 findings into a standing check.
