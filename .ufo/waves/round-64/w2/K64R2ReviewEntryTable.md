## Findings

- **K64R2ReviewEntryTable-1 — HIGH (falsifies `:14-15`, `:28-31`, `:131-133` tree-identity rule).** The entry's central rule ("when the files on disk already equal that commit's tree, and marks the working copy stale when they differ") is false in both directions whenever the seat has unsnapshotted edits on disk: jj compares the **previously recorded working-copy commit's tree** against the new `@` commit's tree before snapshotting disk. Repro: `bash /home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin/kumitate-shokunin/.ufo/waves/round-64/build/K64R2ReviewEntryTable/repro-all.sh | grep R1_TREE_RULE_FALSIFIED` → `disk_ne_new_but_old_eq_new=fresh disk_eq_new_but_old_ne_new=stale`.
- **K64R2ReviewEntryTable-2 — HIGH (falsifies Row 4 `:74` column 2 and guard).** (i) `update-stale` does **not** delete the seat's snapshotted files after `rewrite_treechange` (`squash`); it deletes them only after `abandon`. (ii) A seat that "finds itself stale" **cannot commit** before `update-stale`: `jj commit` exits `1` (`working copy is stale`), `git commit` exits `128` (`seat_has_git_dir=no`), and `jj --ignore-working-copy commit` commits only the empty replacement `@` while `update-stale` still deletes the seat's file. (iii) A change committed *before* staleness (`@-`, committed-but-not-checked-out) survives when `@` is abandoned (`c.txt=present`), but is deleted when `@-` itself is abandoned (`parent_abandon_state=stale`, `c.txt=deleted`). Repro: `bash .../repro-all.sh | grep R2_ROW4_GUARD`.
- **K64R2ReviewEntryTable-3 — HIGH (falsifies Row 6 `:76` citation and guard).** (i) `.ufo/waves/round-64/build/K64ExecJjDemoHarden/measurements-abandon-matrix.txt:15-30` prints `A abandons B head: B=stale` / `B abandons A head: A=fresh` without ever recording that B's abandon errored out (that prose exists only in `w1/K64ExecJjDemoHarden.md:28,40`). (ii) A stale seat **can** act and abandon its peer's head by passing `--ignore-working-copy` (`jj -R "$SB" --ignore-working-copy abandon "$CA"` exits `0` and makes A `stale`). Repro: `bash .../repro-all.sh | grep R3_ROW6_MUTUAL_ABANDON`.
