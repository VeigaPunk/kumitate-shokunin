## Counter findings (not kill-rule events)

1. **HELD without implementing the definitions.** 8 oracle mutants and 10 candidate mutants read
   `C92_C1: HELD agree=133/133`. The gaps are structural stratum gaps (length turns, the 24-char
   boundary, role-tagged first `model_change`, non-object lines / empty `parseerr` stratum, `?` guard,
   all three locate tie-breaks, the lane marker). Additionally the C sample is keyed to the tool's own
   sha256, so a construction can enumerate self-hash variants and keep one whose C draw it passes.
   This is a coverage finding about the instrument; the contract is not touched.
2. **The positive control contradicts the plan text on `captured`.** `plan.md`: fallback to the first
   roleless/`default` row only "if none" (no `subagent:<seat>` row). oracle92.py enters the fallback when
   the subagent row's `model` is not a string. On `fixtures/w1/Seat.jsonl` the candidate (correct per the
   text) reads `captured=null`; the oracle reads `captured="prov/default-model"`, so a definition-faithful
   tool is scored DISAGREE. Not live today: 23139 indexed transcripts carry a `subagent:` row, 0 have a
   non-string model on the first one (`witnesses/subagent-model-scan.txt`). Latent risk for the held-out
   round-92 wave.
3. **Selftest gap with a real cost:** `c17` (cut to 120 then redact) passes all 30 selftest checks and the
   counter, and leaks 20 of 40 credential-shaped characters (`fixtures/wit/capcross.jsonl`). The selftest
   has no case where a 24+ run crosses the 120-char cut; `c01` (24→25) and `c13` (non-object) are caught
   only by the selftest, and `c04/c06/c14/c19` by neither.
