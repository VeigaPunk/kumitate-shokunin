quote (§heartbeat.interval.ms, S118):
"heartbeat.interval.ms — The expected time between heartbeats to the consumer coordinator when using Kafka's group management facilities. Heartbeats are used to ensure that the consumer's session stays active..."
Default: 3000 (3 seconds); must be set lower than session.timeout.ms, typically no higher than 1/3 of that value.
group.consumer.session.timeout.ms default: 45000 (45 seconds).
