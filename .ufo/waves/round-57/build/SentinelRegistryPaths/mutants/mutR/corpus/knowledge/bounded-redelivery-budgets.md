# Bounded redelivery budgets

At-least-once delivery says a unit of work may arrive more than once; it does not say how many times or what happens when the count runs out. This entry is about the *budget* side of redelivery: the counted bound on re-attempts, what the broker does when the bound is reached, and where the lane's own dispatcher already implements the same shape. It is the fifth entry of the durable-execution frontier (after `at-least-once-dedupe.md`, which covers identity, not bounds).

## What the sources ground

- **Kafka decomposes delivery into publish and consume guarantees.** The 4.3 design doc lists at-most-once (may be lost, never redelivered), at-least-once (never lost, may be redelivered) and exactly-once (processed once and only once), then notes this breaks into two problems: the durability of publishing and the guarantees when consuming (S109, §Message delivery semantics). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutKafka/semantics-list.txt@sha256:7caab2a488c5fef50ccac620cbc8eeb5ca567a7db457b9e397bacfca97928891`
- **Exactly-once is a composition, not a delivery mode.** The same section separates the publish-side durability question from consume-side guarantees, so a consumer's redelivery behavior is decided by how its position state is stored, not by the delivery label on the wire (S109). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutKafka/two-problems.txt@sha256:01d086bba5a09fec057ab0ed00a82352d5793ea8684a9da03b5d119e7fdaedd8`
- **A broker-side dedupe needs an identity.** Kafka's idempotent producer assigns each producer an ID and deduplicates messages by it so resending does not duplicate entries — the transport-level analogue of the lane's content-keyed task id (S109). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutKafka/idempotent-producer.txt@sha256:6a1d645050ad8d112f4b548e902e5f4a375ec913872efdae12c5c1b9754a05fe`
- **Commit order chooses redelivery or loss.** Kafka ships at-least-once by default; committing consumer offsets *before* processing buys at-most-once instead — the position checkpoint, not the delivery guarantee, decides whether a failed consumer redelivers or skips (S109). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutKafka/commit-order.txt@sha256:93a0aa3f5d4c33b98c3d8d45d60561cbaea4877dd745d97c0f2f1d6776be864a`
- **Consumer position is explicit state.** A consumer can deliberately rewind its offset to re-consume data; the position is a small, checkpointable integer, so redelivery in Kafka is replay of recorded state rather than broker re-send (S109). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutKafka/offset-rewind.txt@sha256:1eabc18d0ca2dea942a5c414a7cf80d5fbec0418858df6d1f95e6ab8612a1608`
- **Kafka 4.3 is the observed revision.** The design page is the AK 4.3.x documentation stream fetched 2026-10-24; earlier/later version URLs redirect, so this entry does not generalize its wording to other releases. `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutKafka/source-metadata.txt@sha256:111f7bdf6b8adc4637d967623f4464e2ccee8e28a116278ef97ae1d2bfccb7f9`

## Broker-side bounded redelivery

- **RabbitMQ names the redelivery bound directly.** A quorum queue dead-letters a message when it "is returned more times to a quorum queue than the delivery-limit" — the broker counts returns and routes the exhausted delivery to a dead-letter exchange rather than requeueing forever (S110). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutRabbitmqDlx/delivery-limit.txt@sha256:e3923d378240fd7e3c6b64cd58afe91fb893ca2f3d6afe5dbebf950b625ecc36`
- **Dead-lettering is the terminal side effect of a spent budget.** The DLX doc enumerates four trigger events — negative-ack with requeue=false, per-message TTL expiry, queue-length overrun, and delivery-limit exceedance — after which the message is republished to the DLX, taking it out of the redelivery cycle (S110). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutRabbitmqDlx/dlx-events.txt@sha256:68bb4b2d9a668c83b15d138634edb72ad694bb4666a05bb9b659ecd81bc2a7d1`
- **requeue=false is an immediate budget of zero.** A `basic.reject`/`basic.nack` with requeue=false dead-letters at once: the same mechanism that bounds redelivery to N also admits "no redelivery at all" as N=0 (S110). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutRabbitmqDlx/nack-requeue-false.txt@sha256:01cc023dc607d3072357a9fffd6411c1d0191e83a83206526435ef1a18eb0e02`
- **RabbitMQ's DLX page is the observed revision.** The page was fetched 2026-10-24 (67,804 bytes); this entry does not generalize its event list to other RabbitMQ versions or brokers. `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutRabbitmqDlx/source-metadata.txt@sha256:d7949c0b0d2cd0c65db4bc74bac8d61fe9765e69dce8cc14c5895b296ef666c2`

## Client-side bounded retries

- **A retry budget is a resource, not a delay.** AWS's advice is to limit retries *locally* with a token bucket — calls retry while tokens last, then retry at a fixed rate — because unbounded retries amplify load on an already struggling dependency (S111, 2019-12-05). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutAwsBuilders/token-bucket.txt@sha256:07a6f287a10451f8f81977a3c0bbab5bd807a60813f0f5d0351de0a48665fbc4`
- **Jitter decorrelates the retries that remain.** Capped exponential backoff alone makes every client retry at the same capped rate; jitter spreads the remaining retries so a synchronized redelivery storm does not re-create the failure (S111). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutAwsBuilders/backoff-jitter.txt@sha256:bae0511e3b456fb17bc793d36c2c2732f110cadac644b797c03bb145f9f73952`
- **S111 is scoped client-side.** The article is operational guidance from 2019-12-05, fetched 2026-10-24; it grounds retry budgets and jitter for callers, not broker redelivery mechanics. `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutAwsBuilders/source-metadata.txt@sha256:4d00b438adb11530a199b0144b81fa107dd5695fa1906e50055346bf339c069f`

## Mapping to the lane's own budgets

- **The dispatcher already counts a redelivery budget.** `fleet-dispatcher.mjs` caps paused-resume attempts and launch-registration misses at `MAX_PAUSED_RESUME_ATTEMPTS = 8` and `MAX_LAUNCH_MISSES = 8` — the lane's queue has the broker `delivery-limit` shape at its own claim level (origin tree, read-only). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutQueue/resume-budgets.txt@sha256:b5c8dc168ec15985a560f8f32971ed4a21f9e14ebf9bad6873eda2dba727663d`
- **A claim lock has a grace window, not infinite redelivery.** `LAUNCHING_GRACE_MS = 120_000` is the only crash-recovery boundary: a `launching` row older than the window is reclaimable by reconcile — the lane's analogue of a delivery-timeout before re-delivery (origin tree, read-only). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutQueue/launching-grace.txt@sha256:c7504e8acad6d035a3f0478f500493ba48050d0245e39ff67c8105c66ea4a452`
- **A repend is a marked redelivery, not a silent retry.** The watchdog mutates the queue only through `fleet-dispatcher.mjs repend --run <gq16>`; a successful repend stamps `blocker.code='repend_requested'` — redelivery is an explicit, auditable event, mirroring the DLX discipline of routing exhausted deliveries to a named place (origin tree, read-only). `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutQueue/repend-mark.txt@sha256:84f74ca4de1e3648e3037f86a024842365b177a8b94e43ea79cd6d7932907720`
- **[INFERENCE] A redelivery budget needs an observable terminal state.** Combining S110's dead-letter routing with the lane's `blocked`/repend marks: a bounded-redelivery design is only as good as the place exhausted deliveries land — for the lane that place is the `blocked` state and the L0/report surface, not silent drop. This is a design inference drawn from the three sources against the lane's surfaces, not a claim the sources state. `receipt:.ufo/waves/round-55/build/ScoutQueue/inference-terminal-state.txt@sha256:ecbfb23e37814fc1a5403af989593be6b71fa30079de30fa197e90b6182b066b`
