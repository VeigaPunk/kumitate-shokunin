## Cross-Lane Synthesis
1. **Row Recognizer Parity vs Cell Trimming Asymmetry:**
   - Following R98, multiple scripts (`round-gate.mjs:411`, `tabi/check-bidi-runtime.mjs:474`, `tabi/check-decision-table.mjs:122`, `tabi/check-frontier-admission.mjs:126`, and `sources-census.sh:619`) successfully adopted POSIX `blank` `[ \t]` for row recognition (`/^ {0,3}\|[ \t]*S\d{3,}[ \t]*\//`).
   - However, almost ALL of those same tools immediately parse internal cells using `.split('|').map(c => c.trim())` (ECMAScript `String.prototype.trim()`).
2. **Remaining Non-Blank Row Readers in Kumitate:**
   - In `kumitate-shokunin`, `cite-sources.mjs:36` (`/^ {0,3}\|\s*(S[0-9]{3,})\s*\|/`) and `source-capture.mjs:157` (`/^\s*\|\s*(S[0-9]{3,})\s*\|/gm`) still use ECMAScript `\s`.
   - As a consequence, a row padded with NBSP (`| \u00a0S100 |`) is rejected by `round-gate.mjs` and `sources-check.mjs` (R99), but is still recognised as a valid cited row by `cite-sources.mjs` and demands a byte-capture in `source-capture.mjs`.
3. **Fleet Census Inconsistency:**
   - `scripts/sources-census.sh` internally contradicts itself between `row_pass` (`[ \t]`) and `usedby_findings` (`[[:space:]]`), causing locale-dependent E11 findings.
