93: - `tier` — scoped to `scope`, never global. **A**: primary or authoritative for that scope
94:   (spec, official docs, source code, original paper, maintainer statement, the lane's own
95:   re-runnable gate output). **B**: strong secondary (expert write-up with reproducible
96:   detail, survey, textbook). **C**: lead only — never sole support for an admitted entry.
97:   `community` is never A. Tier is curation, not truth: no NATO/GRADE labels on rows.
98: - `scope` — the claim or topic the tier applies to.
99: - `anchor` — exact section / heading / page / line plus revision (version, commit, or
100:   publication date). Never a bare homepage.
101: - `teaches` — one grounded learner benefit. Rejected rows read
102:   `REJECTED: <reason>; flip: <what would change the verdict>`.
103: - `status` — `queued` (opened, worth mining) · `mined` (cited by ≥1 path in `used-by`) ·
104:   `rejected` (kept — a rejection is evidence of taste).
105: - `opened` — ISO date the row's author opened the source. `recheck` — ISO date or cadence
106:   (`90d`, `180d`, `on-release`).
107: - `used-by` — repo-relative paths of the knowledge entries or lessons citing the row,
108:   comma-separated, or `—`.
