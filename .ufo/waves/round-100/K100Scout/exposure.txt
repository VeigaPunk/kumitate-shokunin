Classes measured (per file, one LF-delimited record = one line, gawk RS=LF):
  (i)   a record carrying CR (not immediately before LF), U+2028, U+2029 or U+0085;
  (ii)  an id row whose indent is anything other than 0-3 ASCII SP
        (census row gate `^ {0,3}\|`; the capture reader `^\s*\|` accepts more);
  (iii) a census id cell (field 2) whose pad carries a character other than
        ASCII SP/TAB (the census trims POSIX blank only; the JS readers trim JS \s).
Supplementary:
  (iv)  a capture-reader row found INSIDE an LF record (after CR/U+2028/U+2029),
        i.e. a row the `m`-flag reader sees and the census (RS=LF) does not;
  (v)   reader/census id-set divergence (ids a reader accepts that the census
        does not read as a valid registry id).

Reader regexes under study, asserted verbatim against the lane sources:
  cite-sources.mjs:36    /^ {0,3}\|\s*(S[0-9]{3,})\s*\|/   flags=[]
  source-capture.mjs:157 /^\s*\|\s*(S[0-9]{3,})\s*\|/gm flags=[gm]

Method: K100Scout/scan_exposure.mjs (exact ECMAScript semantics; the two
reader regexes are read out of the lane sources at run time, not transcribed).
Positive control: K100Scout/selftest_scan.mjs -- a synthetic registry carrying
one instance of every class must be flagged, a clean one must not (11/11 PASS).
Model validation: the scanner's census-row model was compared row-for-row with
the live `scripts/sources-census.sh census --file` on all 21 pinned snapshots
(live_census.tsv) -- all 21 match exactly.
