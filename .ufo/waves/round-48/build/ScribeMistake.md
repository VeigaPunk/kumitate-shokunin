## QUALITY recommendation (draft only; not appended to `QUALITY.md`)

Q33–Q35 do not cover either class: Q33 checks SOURCES-row teaching against locators, Q34 checks guard-byte identity, and Q35 checks SOURCES-row immutability (`corpus/QUALITY.md:652-721`). Recommend two criteria because trace completeness and report-evidence provenance are distinct failure modes.

- **Q36 — a counter cannot return a held verdict from an incomplete trace or a below-base exec count.**
  - **Who checks.** The counter author checks the parser and fail-closed branches before freeze; a non-author replays the frozen counter on repeated base runs and checks the per-site counts against the base receipts.
  - **Field.** Every trace record must parse; unfinished/resumed records must pair and their return columns must be recognized. Malformed or unmatched records make the run unevaluable, never held. A site's observed exec count must equal its base count in every scored run; a lower count is a miss/cannot-evaluate, never evidence of ROUTED_HELD.
  - **Fails when.** A held verdict is emitted with an unparsed/unpaired record or with any site count below base.
  - **Shown able to fail.** `.ufo/waves/round-48/probe/receipts/rc-at802-parser.txt:3-16`: the A2 parser loses resumed execs (106/106, 105/106, 103/106 capped); F48 reads 106/106 in the same three logs.
  - **Limit.** A parser can reject visible gaps; this evidence does not establish that strace cannot silently omit an entire traced event under load (round-48 `plan.md:25-26`).

- **Q37 — every close claim is receipt-backed, and every reported aggregate reconciles to its receipts.**
  - **Who checks.** The author links each outcome-affecting run/seat claim to its committed receipt and records run status; a non-author close reviewer checks the claim against the receipt and independently recomputes each stated total. A cancelled-seat or chat-only reading may be described as context, but cannot support a verdict or aggregate.
  - **Field.** For each scored result, record the command, revision, run/seat status, exact result, exit, and committed receipt path. Reconcile counts against the complete cited receipt set. Correct filed errors by an appended erratum, not a silent rewrite.
  - **Fails when.** A conclusion depends on an unreceipted/cancelled-seat reading, a cited receipt contradicts the reported value, or a stated aggregate does not equal the cited results.
  - **Shown able to fail.** `corpus/lessons/round-47-verifier-spotcheck.md:28-29,59-60,183-192` finds that R47's decisive re-runs were only IRC readings and that the claimed all-controls 0/5 conflicts with the 3/5 and 1/5 receipts cited above.
  - **Limit.** Receipt reconciliation verifies what was captured and cited; it does not independently prove that an untrusted receipt faithfully records the claimed process execution.
