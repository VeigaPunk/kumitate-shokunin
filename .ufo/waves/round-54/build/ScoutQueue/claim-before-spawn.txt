`claimNextTask` scans `queue.tasks` in insertion order. Claim flips `pending|paused→launching`, increments attempts, clears the blocker, and `writeQueue` commits BEFORE spawn. A mid-dispatch crash may therefore leave a `launching` row for reconciliation.

The queue's immutable task key survives the claim/dispatch lifecycle; attempt counters describe tries, not operation identity.
