# C2 E4 — capture hash is neither full nor revision-bound

**Counterexample.** E4's intended assertion is that the capture at the measured revision has the digest pinned in that revision's S195 row. The frozen probe does not establish either part. At `.ufo/waves/round-61/probe/doctrine61-probe.mjs:20-21`, it hashes `readFileSync(join(lane,...body.md))` from the live worktree, then accepts `pin.includes(sha.slice(0,16))`. Thus the measured `rev` does not select the bytes, and only a 16-hex prefix is compared. An invented capture can self-pin: put arbitrary bytes in the worktree and put `sha256 <first 16 hex of those bytes>` in the matching row; E4 passes although the alleged source capture has no provenance. The comparison also permits arbitrary trailing pin bytes after the matching prefix.

**Evidence.** The committed source lines above show both defects directly. The lane's round-61 committed source capture hashes to `8ef2ee5f5e196d9d14fc8c85449442764e812f3e4f0f6e2bb1bdc12895f0f8fc` (`git show f351c520bfc4ce193a61145bdfecbcf1cb20db01:.ufo/waves/round-61/build/ScoutSourceWorkerVersioning/body.md | sha256sum`). The exact forgery fixture is durably included in `.ufo/waves/round-61/controls/r61-c2-full-forgery.bundle` (SHA-256 `c5c0154010d5becc752179bee2cbf81c8fb5dc8a8bd7ed8debb2a20ec9f0526b`; bundle verification lists ref `refs/heads/r61-c2-forgery` at `4247d2da937842bd6846874d548b7125b356e14c`). The round-61 spotcheck independently records the forgery's one-line capture hash `15841c50…`, distinct from the genuine `8ef2ee5f…` (`corpus/lessons/round-61-verifier-spotcheck.md:120-127`). The frozen E4 still only compares prefixes, and reads whichever capture exists in its invocation's lane worktree. The source-capture observation establishes differing content, not an E4 pass in isolation: the probe's E3 row lookup is also defective, so this report does not claim to have executed a fully passing forged DOCTRINE61 counter.

**Why this reaches the target.** E4's stated target is capture integrity at a revision. A fabricated digest/capture pair is self-authenticating under the E4 predicate: no trusted digest source exists inside E4, and the worktree is its own authority. Even where other dead checks currently prevent the overall held verdict, they do not repair this independent predicate weakness.

**Impacted axis:** coverage / source-evidence integrity (C2 E4).

**Smallest safe correction:** read capture bytes with `git show <rev>:<capture-path>` and require exact equality between the row's complete 64-hex SHA-256 and the digest of those revision-bound bytes. Keep the actual source-opening evidence separate; a hash match alone cannot prove that bytes came from the claimed URL.
